# Image placeholders

Drop your own images here to replace the on-screen gradient placeholders. All paths are
relative to `public/images/`. Filenames are referenced from `lib/site-config.ts` and the
home components — keep the names (or update `site-config.ts` to match your files).

## Homepage

| Path | Where it shows | Suggested size |
|------|----------------|----------------|
| `hero/hero-portrait.jpg` | Hero editorial portrait | 1200 × 1600 (3:4) |
| `hero/end-rail.jpg` | Closing call-to-action band | 1600 × 1000 |
| `services/service-1.jpg` … `service-5.jpg` | Service preview panel (one per featured service) | 1200 × 1400 |
| `locations/location-1.jpg` … `location-3.jpg` | Clinic location cards | 1000 × 1250 (4:5) |
| `gallery/look-1.jpg` … `look-6.jpg` | "Our Clinics" gallery frames | 1000 × 1400 |
